Life in the rainforest never stops. At five thirty in the morning, the howler monkeys wake up and howl. Their loud call travels very far. People can hear it from kilometers away! Then they look for leaves and fruit to eat.

During the day, toucans fly from tree to tree and eat fruit. Hummingbirds visit hundreds of flowers and feed on nectar. Sloths usually rest high in the trees. They move very slowly, and they climb down to the ground only about once a week.

When the sun goes down, the rainforest changes. Bats leave their caves and hunt insects. Owls open their big eyes and hunt mice. Frogs sing near the river, and jaguars walk quietly through the forest. Nocturnal animals sleep during the day and look for food at night.

Every animal has its own routine. Some animals are diurnal and some are nocturnal, but all of them are part of the rainforest.
