Marta: Let's play Guess the Animal!

Pedro: OK. How do we play?

Marta: First, I choose an animal. I don't tell you the name! Then, I describe its habits. You have three chances to guess.

Pedro: Easy. Start!

Marta: This animal is nocturnal. It sleeps during the day, and it hunts insects at night. It can fly.

Pedro: Is it an owl?

Marta: No! Owls hunt mice. This animal sleeps upside down.

Pedro: Oh! It's a bat!

Marta: Yes! Your turn.

Pedro: OK. This animal is diurnal. It's very small, and it feeds on nectar. It visits hundreds of flowers every day.

Marta: A hummingbird!

Pedro: Correct!
