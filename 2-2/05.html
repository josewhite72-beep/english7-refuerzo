Last Saturday, our class went to the rainforest in Gamboa with our science teacher.

We woke up very early and arrived at six in the morning. First, we heard the howler monkeys. They were really loud!

Then we observed toucans eating fruit in a tall tree.

At noon, it was very hot, and most animals were resting. Our guide showed us a sloth. It didn't move for one hour!

In the afternoon, we saw hundreds of hawks in the sky. The guide said they migrate from North America to South America every October.

We went home at five, before the bats came out. It was the best trip of the year!
