Guía: Welcome to the Metropolitan Natural Park! This park is a habitat for many animals.

Carlos: Are there monkeys here?

Guía: Yes, there are. You can see them in the morning.

Carlos: What is that animal in the tree?

Guía: That's a sloth. Sloths are the slowest mammals in the forest. They eat leaves.

Carlos: Is it dangerous?

Guía: No, it isn't. But it has long claws. Look, there is a toucan, too!

Carlos: Wow! The toucan is more colorful than the sloth.

Guía: Yes! Remember: don't feed the animals, and don't touch them. We must protect their habitat.
