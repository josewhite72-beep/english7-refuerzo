Ábrelo solo después de responder.

Rosa: Hi, Luis! How was your first week?

Luis: It was good, thanks. But this school is very big!

Rosa: What was your previous school like?

Luis: It was small and quiet. There were only six classrooms.

Rosa: Only six? Wow! Did you have a library?

Luis: No, we didn't. But we had a big garden. We planted tomatoes and corn.

Rosa: That's cool. How did you go to school?

Luis: I used to walk with my cousin. Now my mom drives me.

Rosa: Do you like this school?

Luis: Yes! The computer room is the best. And next month, I will join the science club.

Rosa: Me too! See you there.
