Hi, I'm Elena. I'm from Antón, in Coclé.

My old school was in the countryside, near a river. It was a small school with only forty students.

We had classes from seven to twelve, and then I went home for lunch. Every Friday, we cleaned the school garden together.

Now I study in Penonomé. My new school has five hundred students, so it is much bigger and noisier.

Classes start at seven and finish at one thirty, and we eat lunch in the cafeteria.

The best thing about my new school is the music room. I used to play the guitar alone at home, but now I play in the school band.

Next year, I will be in the school concert!
