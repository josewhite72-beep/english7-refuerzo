My name is Luis Castillo. Last year, my family moved from Las Tablas to Colón. My previous school was small and old. There were only six classrooms, and every classroom had a big window. The school had a garden, and we planted tomatoes and corn there. I used to walk to school with my cousin, and we talked about soccer all the way.

My new school is bigger and more modern than my old school. It has a computer room and a large library. The cafeteria is the noisiest place in the school! At first, I was nervous because I didn't know anyone. Then a classmate named Rosa helped me find my classroom. Now we are good friends.

I miss my old garden, but I like my new school. Next month, I will join the science club. Maybe we will make a garden here too!
